Where we ship

Rosalia currently ships to:

  • United States (all 50 states, plus military APO/FPO addresses)
  • Canada
  • United Kingdom
  • European Union (select countries — see checkout for real-time availability)

If your country isn't listed at checkout, we don't ship there yet. Email contact@try-evena.com if you'd like us to expand to your region.

Processing time

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days. Orders placed on weekends or holidays ship the next business day.

Shipping methods and delivery times

United States

  • Standard Shipping — free on orders $50+, otherwise $6.95. 3–7 business days.
  • Expedited Shipping — $14.95. 2–3 business days.

Canada

  • Standard Shipping — $9.95. 5–10 business days. Customs/duties may apply.

United Kingdom & EU

  • Standard Shipping — $12.95. 7–14 business days. Customs/duties may apply and are the customer's responsibility.

Tracking

You'll receive a tracking number by email once your order ships. You can also track your order anytime on our Track Your Order page. If you don't receive your tracking number within 3 business days of ordering, check your spam folder or contact us.

Lost or delayed orders

If your tracking hasn't updated in 7+ business days, or if the tracker shows "delivered" but you haven't received the package, email us at contact@try-evena.com. We'll open an investigation with the carrier and, if the package is confirmed lost, ship a replacement at no cost.

Wrong address

If you enter the wrong shipping address at checkout, contact us within 4 hours and we'll try to intercept the order. After 4 hours, we can't guarantee we can catch it before it ships.
