Deploy the ZIP

unzip sofatoo-site-light.zip -d sofatoo
cd sofatoo
npx netlify-cli deploy --dir=. --prod

Or drag the unzipped folder onto app.netlify.com/drop. Works the same on Vercel, Cloudflare Pages or any classic host.

Change things

Colours and fonts: assets/mockup/overrides.css
Currency, labels, demo badge: assets/mockup/shop-demo.js (CONFIG at the top)
Everything else: CUSTOMIZE.md inside the ZIP.
