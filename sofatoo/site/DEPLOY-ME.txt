# So' Fatoo — deploy this folder

Drag this whole folder onto https://app.netlify.com/drop — it goes live as-is.
No build, no Node, no config. Or:

    npx netlify-cli deploy --dir=. --prod
    npx vercel deploy . --prod
    npx wrangler pages deploy .

Or upload the contents into public_html/ on any classic host.

To change colours/fonts: assets/mockup/overrides.css
To change currency/labels: assets/mockup/shop-demo.js (CONFIG at the top)
Full guide: CUSTOMIZE.md
