SO’ FATOO · DAKAR

An African fashion house shaped by memory, identity and the quiet confidence of being entirely yourself.

01

So’ Fatoo

Launched in Morocco in 2012 and established in Senegal, So’ Fatoo designs, produces and shares authentic, elegant and inclusive clothing inspired by Africa and made in Africa, with the Senegalese woven loincloth at its heart.

02

A name with a story

So’ Fatou—or Sow Fatou—is a play on words that brings together the founder’s grandmother, namesake and muse with a declaration that means ‘very Fatou’: unmistakably West African.

03

Reclaiming an identity

Fatou is one of West Africa’s most familiar women’s names. So’ Fatoo carries it with pride: strong, refined and resolutely elegant, as a symbol of African culture and self-affirmation.

04

Woven into every silhouette

Our designs celebrate Senegalese woven cloth, an essential part of our cultural heritage. Comfortable and expressive, every cut carries forward a rich tradition of elegance.
