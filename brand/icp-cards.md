# ICP Cards — PHOENIX VOLETS

> VOC phrases below are drawn from Guinean press reporting on break-ins (Guineenews, Africaguinee, Guineematin, Libreopinion, 2024–2026) and from expat/resident security guidance. They are **representative category language**, not quotes from Phoenix customers.
> **Rule for creative:** these may be used as *narration or category observation*. They may **NOT** be set as a fake five-star customer testimonial until Phoenix supplies real, consented reviews. See brand-dna.md §5.

---

## ICP 1 — "Le propriétaire de villa" (PRIMARY)

**Who:** 38–60, Conakry (Kipé, Nongo, Lambanyi, Cosa, Kaloum, Sonfonia). Cadre, commerçant établi, fonctionnaire supérieur, profession libérale. Owns, did not rent. Has a wall, a gate, probably a vigile.
**Household:** spouse + 2–5 children, often extended family. Frequently the one everyone else depends on.

**Core pain**
- The wall and the gate are already there — and the break-in still happened to someone on the same street.
- The weak point is not the door. It is the window, the louvre, the thin metal.
- Travels for work; the house sits with women and children in it.
- Has already paid for a vigile and discovered a vigile sleeps.

**What they actually fear (unspoken):** not losing the TV. Being the man who was not there.

**VOC phrases**
- « j'ouvre la porte, je constate que la voiture a disparu »
- « ils sont passés par le mur »
- « ils ont déchiré la tôle »
- « on n'a rien entendu »
- « même avec le vigile »
- « c'était en pleine nuit »

**Objections**
1. "C'est cher." → Reframe against the recurring cost of a vigile (≈ 50–80 $/mois) and against one night's loss.
2. "Est-ce que ça résiste vraiment ?" → Material specificity, CE certification, not adjectives.
3. "Et si ça tombe en panne ? Qui répare ?" → SAV + pièces disponibles localement. This is the real decider in Guinea.
4. "Ça va rouiller avec l'humidité." → Tropical adaptation, origine France.
5. "Je ferai ça plus tard." → The only honest counter is that the decision is always made after, never before.

**Awareness:** Problem-aware → Solution-aware. Needs mechanism, not education on burglary.

## ICP 2 — "Le commerçant" (HIGHEST URGENCY)

**Who:** 28–55, boutique / dépôt / atelier / quincaillerie / pharmacie. Madina, Matoto, Kaloum, Sonfonia, marché de Taouyah. Stock *is* the capital.
**Reality:** closes at night and leaves the entire business behind a thin shutter.

**Core pain**
- One night can erase everything. Reported cases: an entire sewing workshop emptied overnight; 35 million GNF of goods taken in a single Cosa break-in.
- The documented method: climb the wall, then **cut the sheet metal with a chisel and hammer**.
- No stock insurance. No recovery. The loss is final.
- Reopening means re-buying stock he has already paid for once.

**VOC phrases**
- « ils ont tout emporté »
- « le matin, la boutique était vide »
- « ils ont forcé avec un burin »
- « j'ai tout perdu en une nuit »
- « il a fallu racheter le stock »

**Objections**
1. "Je ferme avec un cadenas, ça suffit." → The padlock is never the failure point; the panel is.
2. "Ça coûte le prix de mon stock." → It costs a fraction of *one* emptied night.
3. "La pose va fermer ma boutique." → Install timing, measured lead time **[À VÉRIFIER]**.
4. "Je suis locataire du local." → Removable/transferable installation.

**Awareness:** Most-aware after any incident nearby. This segment converts on urgency; it does not need persuading, it needs a number to call.

## ICP 3 — "Le bâtisseur de la diaspora" (HIGHEST TICKET)

**Who:** 30–55 Guinean living in France, Belgium, US, Canada, Gulf. Building or finishing a house in Conakry. Sends money monthly for a project he sees only on WhatsApp photos.
**Context:** the house is the life project, the retirement plan, the family's standing — and he is 5 000 km away from it.

**Core pain**
- Pays for everything, controls nothing.
- Has been burned on materials before: paid for quality, received uncertified metal that rusted in one rainy season.
- Cannot supervise. Relies on a brother, a cousin, a foreman.
- The house often sits empty or half-finished for months — the single most attractive target there is.
- Comes home once a year and discovers what was actually installed.

**What they actually want:** proof. Photos, certification, a company that still exists next year and has parts on the shelf.

**VOC phrases**
- « j'envoie l'argent chaque mois »
- « je ne vois que les photos »
- « on m'a dit que c'était de la qualité »
- « ça a rouillé en une saison des pluies »
- « la maison est vide en attendant »

**Objections**
1. "Comment je paie depuis l'étranger ?" → Payment path **[À VÉRIFIER]**.
2. "Comment je sais que c'est bien posé ?" → Photo/video report of the chantier. Strong differentiator.
3. "Qui fait le SAV quand je ne suis pas là ?" → Local SAV, parts in stock.
4. "On va me vendre de la camelote." → CE certificate, origine France, assemblé localement.

**Awareness:** Solution-aware, **brand-sceptical**. This segment is won on *proof and traceability*, not on fear. Do not sell this persona fear — sell it control.

## ICP 4 — "Le gestionnaire" (B2B, secondary)

**Who:** office manager / directeur administratif of an SME, NGO, clinic, school, bank branch, telecom site.
**Pain:** responsible for assets and for staff safety; needs an invoice, a warranty and a maintenance contract — not a vibe.
**Trigger:** an incident at a comparable site (ex. equipment stolen from a telecom operator's premises in Conakry).
**Decides on:** compliance, documentation, SAV contract, references **[À VÉRIFIER]**.
**Register:** no fear. Institutional, factual, procurement-friendly.

## Cross-ICP creative notes

- **The universal insight:** everyone already has *something* — a wall, a gate, a padlock, a vigile. Nobody is unprotected. They are **under**-protected at one specific point. The ads must attack that point, not the absence of security.
- **The universal objection to beat:** not price. It is **"qui répare quand ça casse ?"** — the Guinean market is full of orphaned installations with no parts. SAV + pièces disponibles is the strongest asset Phoenix owns. Use it more than price.
- **Rhythm of the market:** rainy season (May–Oct) drives rust/corrosion anxiety; Ramadan/Tabaski and year-end drive both shop stock peaks and break-in peaks.
- **Channel:** WhatsApp. The CTA is a conversation, not a form.
